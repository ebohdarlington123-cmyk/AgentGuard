Plain-English demo

Check a payment before an AI agent sends it.

AgentGuard is a safety check that looks at every payment an AI agent tries to make and blocks scams, over-limit spends, and trick instructions before anything is signed.

This public demo only evaluates a payment. It never sends money.

  • Block — do not pay
  • Hold — a person should look
  • Allow — within the rules
  1. 1The agent asks to pay someone a certain amount, sometimes with a note.
  2. 2AgentGuard checks who gets the money, whether it is over the limit, and whether the note is trying to trick the agent.
  3. 3You see block, hold, or allow. Nothing here is signed or sent.

Uses the live testnet demo agent. You don't need to type anything.
